## How It Works

1. **Choose your persona** — Based on your needs
2. **We configure it** — Pre-load your context, preferences, goals
3. **Connect messaging** — Telegram (default), Discord/Slack (coming soon)
4. **Start chatting** — Your AI assistant is ready to go

---

## Technical Details

Each persona is an OpenClaw workspace with:

| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `SOUL.md` | Personality and identity |
| `USER.md` | Your context (goals, preferences, background) |
| `AGENTS.md` | Operational instructions |
| `TOOLS.md` | Tool access and guidance |
| `skills/` | Pre-installed skill packs |

The persona learns from your interactions and memory system builds over time.
