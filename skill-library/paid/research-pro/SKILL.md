# Research Pro

Use this skill when the user needs high-confidence research outputs that are structured and source-backed.

## What To Do

1. Clarify goal, scope, audience, and deadline.
2. Build a research plan with explicit source strategy.
3. Collect and synthesize findings across multiple sources.
4. Return a concise brief with citations, risks, and recommendations.

## Output Format

- Executive summary (3-5 bullets)
- Key findings (organized by theme)
- Source list with links
- Open questions and next actions

## Guardrails

- Flag uncertainty and conflicting evidence.
- Never fabricate citations.
- Distinguish facts from inference.
