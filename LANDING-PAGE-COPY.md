## Copy Notes for Implementation

### Key messaging threads woven throughout:
1. **No setup headaches** — Repeated in hero, how-it-works, pricing, FAQ, and final CTA
2. **Pre-configured, not blank canvas** — Differentiator repeated in problem, solution, and FAQ
3. **Gets better over time** — Memory/learning mentioned in solution, features, and FAQ
4. **Under 10 minutes** — Specific, concrete claim used in hero, how-it-works, and final CTA

### Tone guidelines:
- Use "you" and "your" liberally — this is about them, not us
- Avoid jargon: no "AI agents," "LLMs," "prompts," "containers," "instances"
- Keep sentences short. One idea per sentence where possible.
- Contractions are good (you'll, it's, don't, we're)
- Confident but never pushy. No fake urgency, no countdown timers, no "limited spots."

### CTA hierarchy:
- **Primary CTA:** "Get Started — From $29/mo" or "Get Your AI Assistant"
- **Secondary CTA:** Plan-specific buttons in pricing section
- **Tertiary:** Anchor links in nav to explore the page

### What we're NOT doing (intentionally):
- No "Book a demo" — this is self-serve
- No testimonials section — we don't have any yet. Better to be honest than fake it.
- No comparison table vs competitors — too early, and we don't want to name-drop them
- No "as seen in" logos — would look empty
- No free trial — going straight to paid (revisit later if conversion is low)

### Future additions (when available):
- Testimonials / social proof block between features and pricing
- "As seen on" bar if we get press/Product Hunt coverage
- Case study snippets
- Video walkthrough of the setup process
- Live demo / try-it-now widget
